Current role · September 2025 to present

Cognitif Group

An AI behavioural-data and psychological measurement company. I own the frontend: the participant-facing product, the on-device logic behind it, and the company's public site.

i.

The participant frontend, end to end

I led design and engineering of the entire participant-facing product: a real-time interactive data-capture interface. Every screen, every interaction, and the on-device processing that turns a live session into usable data. It has to feel effortless to the person using it and stay precise enough for the measurement to mean anything.

ii.

Anonymous by architecture

I worked directly on how the product handles data. Individual session data is processed and discarded on the participant's device, so nothing personally identifiable is retained. Anonymity is a property of how the system is built, not a policy written over the top of it.

iii.

cognitifgroup.com

I designed and built the company website from scratch. Visual identity, animation system, copy, structure, and deployment. It is the reference point for how the company presents itself.

Founder · Live on the App Store

Goalsreach

A self improvement app built around accountability. Momentum comes from turning up, not from being pushed. I made all of it.

i.

Accountability, not pressure

Most goal apps punish you for missing a day, and people quit the moment the streak breaks. Goalsreach is built the other way round. It tracks consistency, keeps the record honest, and makes coming back the easy thing to do.

ii.

Built end to end in SwiftUI

Native iOS, written in SwiftUI. Every screen, the data model underneath it, and the release. No template, no framework doing the thinking.

iii.

Live on iOS, and goalsreach.co.uk

Shipped to the App Store, with the identity and the website designed alongside it. The product and the brand were made by the same person, and it shows in both.
